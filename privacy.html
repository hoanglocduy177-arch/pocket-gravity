Privacy Policy

Pocket Gravity Uploader · Last updated: 7 November 2026

1. Summary

Pocket Gravity Uploader ("the app") is a personal desktop tool used only by the owner of the Pocket Gravity TikTok account. It does not collect data from anyone else.

2. Data the app uses

  • TikTok sign-in tokens (access token, refresh token, open ID) for the owner's own account, received through TikTok Login Kit. They are stored only on the owner's computer and used only to post the owner's videos.
  • Basic profile info of the owner's account (display name, avatar) and the posting options TikTok returns, used to show who is posting and which settings are allowed.
  • Video files and captions chosen by the owner, which are sent to TikTok to be published.

3. What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, sell or share personal data of other TikTok users.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • It does not send data to any server other than TikTok's official APIs.

4. Storage and deletion

Tokens are kept in a local file on the owner's computer. The owner can delete them at any time, and can revoke the app's access in TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions).

5. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date above shows the latest version.
