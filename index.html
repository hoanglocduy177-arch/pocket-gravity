Pocket Gravity Uploader

A personal desktop tool for the Pocket Gravity creator.

Pocket Gravity makes short, satisfying physics simulation videos. Every video is generated entirely in code: balls escaping spinning rings, growing, racing and multiplying, with every bounce playing a note.

Pocket Gravity Uploader is a small desktop program used only by the owner of the Pocket Gravity account. It lets the owner sign in with TikTok (Login Kit) and publish their own finished simulation videos to their own TikTok account using the TikTok Content Posting API, with a caption and privacy setting chosen by the owner.

What it does

  • Signs the owner in with TikTok to get permission to post on their behalf.
  • Reads the owner's basic profile info and the posting options TikTok allows.
  • Uploads a video file from the owner's computer and publishes it with the owner's caption.

It does not collect data from other TikTok users, and it is not offered to the public.
